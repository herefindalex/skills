# Company & Role Investigator

Company and role research for interview preparation.

Company & Role Investigator helps you understand a company, interpret a job description, and prepare questions that test what a role may involve. It separates sourced claims, inferences, alternative explanations, and unknowns.

## What it helps you do

- Research a company's products, customers, business context, and public information.
- Interpret a role's responsibilities, organizational position, and possible decision rights.
- Form evidence-based hypotheses about challenges the role may need to address.
- Prepare neutral interview questions and revise the assessment after new information.

## How to use it

Install the plugin in a supported ChatGPT or Codex environment, then start a conversation with the plugin enabled. Provide the company name, official website, and job description or public posting URL.

For example:

> Investigate this company and role. Explain the company and product, the role's likely responsibilities, and the challenges I should validate in an interview. Distinguish sources, inferences, alternatives, and unknowns. Company: [name]. Website: [URL]. Job description: [text or URL].

Responses can follow the language you request. Research depends on the sources and tools available in your host environment; some questions may remain unresolved. Verify important conclusions against current sources and the employer's answers.

## How the plugin works

This is a Skills-only plugin containing instructions, reference materials, and a report template. It has no MCP server, publisher-operated backend, or analytics. The publisher does not receive your research conversations through the plugin. ChatGPT, Codex, search services, and websites have their own data practices; see the privacy policy below.
