# Company & Role Investigator Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026  
Publisher: Alex Chang  
Contact: [herefindalex@gmail.com](mailto:herefindalex@gmail.com)

## Scope

Company & Role Investigator provides research instructions, reference material, and report templates for an assistant running in ChatGPT or Codex. Version 0.1.0 is a Skills-only plugin. It has no MCP server, publisher-operated backend, connected-account integration, lifecycle hook, analytics, or research-history database.

## Research information

The assistant uses the company name, job description, public sources, and any additional material you choose to provide. That material may include a resume or interview notes.

The publisher does not independently receive, collect, store, sell, or share your research inputs, conversations, attachments, or generated reports through this plugin. The plugin has no publisher-operated endpoint to which that information is sent.

The research instructions do not authorize reading private mailboxes or cloud files. They instruct the assistant to keep private resumes, salary details, interview notes, and contact information out of public search queries. They do not authorize sending messages, submitting job applications, or writing research to external services.

## Host platforms and public-source services

Research takes place in the host assistant. ChatGPT, Codex, and any search or browsing services you use may process or retain inputs, outputs, and queries under their own terms, account settings, and privacy policies. Public-source services may receive the public research queries or page requests needed to retrieve their content.

This plugin does not control those services' retention, model-training settings, deletion, or access controls. It does not promise that processing stays on your device. Manage conversation history, attachments, and deletion through the host platform. See the [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) for OpenAI's practices.

## Support email

If you contact the publisher by email, the publisher receives your email address and the contents you send, through the email service provider. This information is used to respond to your request and provide related follow-up. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

Support correspondence is retained for the time needed to handle the request and related follow-up. You may request deletion by emailing [herefindalex@gmail.com](mailto:herefindalex@gmail.com). Information that must be retained to meet applicable legal obligations may be kept for that purpose. The email provider's own processing and retention are governed by its policies.

## This policy website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. The publisher does not add analytics, tracking scripts, forms, or advertising to this policy page. GitHub may process standard website request information under its own [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Hosting this page does not give the publisher access to your assistant conversations.

## Retention and user controls

There is no publisher-operated store of research history to retain or delete. Conversation and attachment controls belong to the host platform. For information you send directly by support email, use the contact above to request access or deletion.

## Changes

This policy describes the Skills-only functionality above. If a future version adds a backend, account integration, telemetry, or data storage, the policy will be updated before that functionality is distributed. Changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
