# Research Report Structure

This is an output contract, not a mandatory form. Replace these instructions with natural prose rather than repeating the template itself. For company-only requests, omit role-specific sections. Combine sections to meet the user's length preference while retaining evidence and uncertainty.

## 1. Research scope

Company, product or unit, target role, research date or cutoff, and JD version. Explain source or tool limitations early when they affect conclusions. Clearly label fictional or fixed-material tests rather than presenting them as real company research.

## 2. Core interpretation

The two or three most useful findings. Distinguish facts, statements, and inferences within the prose, with nearby sources. Exploratory hypotheses are not established conclusions. Address the work the user cares about before accumulating company history.

## 3. Company and product

Who uses and pays, what problem is solved, how value is delivered, and which directions create demands relevant to the role. Leave unknowns unknown.

## 4. Role position

Outcome responsibility, authority, resources, collaboration, and success conditions. Include related hiring and events only when they explain the work; do not present them as a complete organization chart.

## 5. Main challenges and hypotheses

For each, follow the [Hypothesis card](../references/role-and-challenge-analysis.md): hypothesis, evidence, work implications, alternative explanation or counterevidence, support level, and validation question. Usually provide two to four, or fewer with limited evidence. Generic JD requirements alone do not establish company dysfunction.

## 6. Interview validation

Usually three to five questions, each linked to a hypothesis. Explain which answers would support or weaken it. Do not make every question point toward the same predetermined conclusion.

## 7. Unknowns and source limitations

Unconfirmed points, unread originals, conflicts, and source scope. Do not hide important limitations until the end or place all sources in an isolated list of links.

For follow-up updates, replace unnecessary background with original interpretation, new evidence, revision, and remaining question.
