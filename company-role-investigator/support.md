# Company & Role Investigator Support

Publisher: Alex Chang.

For questions, installation problems, bug reports, or privacy requests, email [herefindalex@gmail.com](mailto:herefindalex@gmail.com).

## Reporting a problem

Please include:

- The plugin version and whether you are using ChatGPT or Codex.
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
- A short example or screenshot with private information removed, if helpful.

Do not send passwords, API keys, private resumes, confidential interview notes, or other unnecessary sensitive information. A public company name or posting URL is usually sufficient for a research example.

Support is provided by email as available. There is no guaranteed response time. For account access, billing, or host-platform problems, contact that platform's support team.

## Privacy requests

The plugin has no publisher-operated research-history store. Manage conversations and attachments through your host platform. If you have sent the publisher support email, you may use the same address to request access to or deletion of that correspondence, as described in the [privacy policy]({{ '/company-role-investigator/privacy-policy/' | relative_url }}).
